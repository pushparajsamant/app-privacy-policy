    <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
    <p class="last-updated">Last updated: 12 May 2026</p>

    <p>
      Welcome to <strong>Taskly Kids</strong> ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains
      how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application
      and any related services (collectively, the "App"). Please read this policy carefully. If you
      disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the App.
    </p>

    <hr />

    <h2>1. Who We Are</h2>
    <p>
      Taskly Kids is developed and operated by Pushparaj Samant. If you have questions about this
      policy, see the <a href="#contact">Contact Us</a> section at the bottom of this page.
    </p>

    <h2>2. Information We Collect</h2>

    <p>We collect information in the following ways:</p>

    <h2>2a. Information You Provide Directly</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>
        <strong>Account information:</strong> email address and password when you register a parent
        account.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Family profile:</strong> family name and the first names (or nicknames) you assign
        to children's profiles.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>App content:</strong> tasks, rewards, point values, and approval data you create
        within the App.
      </li>
    </ul>

    <h2>2b. Information Collected Automatically</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>
        <strong>Usage data:</strong> interactions with app features (e.g., tasks completed, rewards
        redeemed) used to provide core functionality.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Device identifiers:</strong> anonymous device or installation identifiers used for
        session management and crash diagnostics.
      </li>
    </ul>

    <h2>2c. Payment &amp; Subscription Information</h2>
    <p>
      Taskly Kids offers optional premium features via an in-app subscription. All payment
      processing is handled by Apple (via the App Store) and <strong>RevenueCat</strong> (our
      subscription management provider). We do <strong>not</strong> collect, store, or process
      credit card or payment details directly. RevenueCat may collect anonymous subscriber
      identifiers and purchase receipts to verify subscription status. See
      <a href="https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener"
        >RevenueCat's Privacy Policy</a
      >
      for details.
    </p>

    <h2>3. How We Use Your Information</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>To create and manage your account and family profiles.</li>
      <li>To provide, operate, and improve the App's features.</li>
      <li>To verify subscription status and unlock premium features.</li>
      <li>To respond to your support requests or inquiries.</li>
      <li>To ensure the security and integrity of our services.</li>
      <li>To comply with applicable legal obligations.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>We do <strong>not</strong> sell your personal information to third parties.</p>

    <h2>4. Children's Privacy</h2>
    <p>
      Taskly Kids is designed for use by parents and guardians to manage tasks and rewards for their
      children. Children do not register their own accounts. A parent or guardian creates and
      controls all child profiles, which contain only a first name or nickname.
    </p>
    <p>
      We do <strong>not</strong> knowingly collect personal information directly from children under
      the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe
      we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can
      delete it.
    </p>
    <p>
      If your child uses the App through a child profile set up by you, you acknowledge that you are
      providing consent on behalf of your child in accordance with applicable laws, including the
      Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK Children's Code where applicable.
    </p>

    <h2>5. Data Storage &amp; Security</h2>
    <p>
      Your account and app data are stored securely using <strong>Supabase</strong>, a hosted
      backend-as-a-service platform. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest. Supabase
      is SOC 2 compliant. See
      <a href="https://supabase.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener"
        >Supabase's Privacy Policy</a
      >
      for more information.
    </p>
    <p>
      While we implement industry-standard safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100%
      secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.
    </p>

    <h2>6. Third-Party Services</h2>
    <p>The App integrates the following third-party services that may process your data:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>
        <strong>Supabase</strong> – authentication and database hosting.
        <a href="https://supabase.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Privacy Policy</a>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>RevenueCat</strong> – subscription and in-app purchase management.
        <a href="https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener"
          >Privacy Policy</a
        >
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Apple App Store</strong> – app distribution and payment processing.
        <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"
          >Privacy Policy</a
        >
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Expo / EAS</strong> – over-the-air update delivery.
        <a href="https://expo.dev/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Privacy Policy</a>
      </li>
    </ul>
    <p>
      We do not share your data with these providers beyond what is necessary to deliver our
      services.
    </p>

    <h2>7. Data Retention</h2>
    <p>
      We retain your account and app data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your
      account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except
      where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.
    </p>

    <h2>8. Your Rights</h2>
    <p>
      Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Access:</strong> request a copy of the data we hold about you.</li>
      <li><strong>Correction:</strong> ask us to correct inaccurate information.</li>
      <li><strong>Deletion:</strong> request that we delete your account and associated data.</li>
      <li>
        <strong>Portability:</strong> receive your data in a portable format where technically
        feasible.
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Objection / Restriction:</strong> object to or request restriction of certain
        processing activities.
      </li>
    </ul>
    <p>To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.</p>

    <h2>9. International Transfers</h2>
    <p>
      Your data may be stored and processed in data centres outside your country of residence
      (including the United States and the European Union) by our third-party providers. Where such
      transfers occur, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the
      provider's own compliance frameworks (e.g., Supabase's EU data region options).
    </p>

    <h2>10. Changes to This Policy</h2>
    <p>
      We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by
      updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an
      in-app notification. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of
      the revised policy.
    </p>
